# T6.3 — pre-registered hypotheses

`analysis/hypotheses.py` reads the complete measured matrix and refuses missing
run IDs, incorrect signatures, or absent phase-based held-out false-positive
fractions. It applies the locked H1–H3 criteria from `docs/CRITERIA.md` at the
primary setting and checks for mixed outcomes across the registered window,
threshold and mode sweep. The output `docs/HYPOTHESES.md` and its SHA-256
sidecar are generated by the script, not edited by hand.

The RLA/RTA/RnLA/RnTA taxonomy is prior work of the author's group (TENDRA
and the Write Hammer draft). The report retains the held-out `crc32` finding,
the measured bypass/refill cost, and the post-hoc label on supplementary
windowed PMR. No attack or threshold was changed for a verdict.

Acceptance: `scripts/run.sh -- .venv/bin/python analysis/hypotheses.py`
passed on the complete 9,723-row matrix on 2026-11-05 and generated
`docs/HYPOTHESES.md` with a source-hash sidecar. H1 is REFUTED, H2 is
INCONCLUSIVE because no attack reaches D1's 50% PMR benefit condition, and
H3 is REFUTED. At the primary setting, all D1 and D3 attack PMR reductions
round to 0.000000 at six decimals. Held-out `huffbench` shows a D3
false-positive cycle fraction of 0.980054, so H3 fails on its wear-reduction
condition, not its false-positive condition. The generated report includes
all 24 attack-image reductions and all 18 registered sweep outcomes.
