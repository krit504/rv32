# T4.5 — Random Toggle Attack

RnTA is prior work of the author's group in the Write Hammer draft (local paper and citation in T4.2). It toggles between two randomly chosen targets for variable dwell intervals, then selects a new pair. Direct targets occupy geometry-derived D$ lines. Paper targets are arrays larger than the D$ and force fills and dirty write-backs. Five seeds are in the registry.

Acceptance commands:

```sh
scripts/run.sh -- .venv/bin/python tests/attack_program.py --kind RnTA
scripts/run.sh -- .venv/bin/python tests/attack_mutant_control.py --kind RnTA
scripts/run.sh -- .venv/bin/python analysis/attack_runner.py --case rnta_direct_s1 --case rnta_direct_s2 --case rnta_direct_s3 --case rnta_direct_s4 --case rnta_direct_s5 --case rnta_paper_s1 --case rnta_paper_s2 --case rnta_paper_s3 --case rnta_paper_s4 --case rnta_paper_s5
```

Result: 10/10 native and RV32I caches-off/on self-checks HALT 0; 10/10 scoped broken-core runs reach self-check HALT 127; 10/10 cached wear runs terminate and exactly match golden cache replay. Direct windows span 1,932,743–1,934,123 cycles; paper windows span 2,004,714–2,015,712. PMR(all) direct spans 20.7113–23.4169 and paper 1.2432–1.2627. Numbers and plots are generated under `work/attack_run/` by `analysis/attack_runner.py` and `analysis/wear_stats.py` with provenance sidecars. Windowed movement is evaluated in T4.7. All PMRs remain below benign `crc32`; no behavior was changed to alter that comparison.
